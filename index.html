Meet the mountains of northern Pakistan.

Nine destinations, planned around the season. Go for blossoms in spring, green meadows in summer, golden leaves in autumn or snow in winter.

Where we take you

Nine places, each with the months when it looks its best.

Gilgit-Baltistan

Hunza Valley

Terraced orchards sit below snow peaks, and Rakaposhi glows pink at sunrise. Old forts, quiet villages and clear air make it the calmest stop in the north.

  • April apricot and cherry blossom
  • Jun to Aug fruit season and warm days
  • October golden autumn leaves

Gilgit-Baltistan

Skardu

Turquoise lakes, a cold desert with sand dunes and the wide Deosai Plains, all under the Karakoram. Shangrila Lake is the quiet highlight.

  • Jun to Sep Deosai Plains open, wildflowers
  • October autumn colour around the lakes
  • Dec to Feb snow over the valley and desert

Gilgit-Baltistan

Fairy Meadows

A short trek through pine forest ends at a green meadow right under Nanga Parbat. The peak turns gold at sunset and the night sky is full of stars.

  • May to Jun fresh grass and cool trek
  • Jul to Sep best weather and clear views
  • Nov to Apr deep snow, for experienced trekkers

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Swat Valley

Often called the Switzerland of the East, with rivers, deodar forests and wide meadows. Kalam and Ushu forest stay cool even in summer.

  • Mar to Apr blossom in the lower valley
  • Jun to Sep Kalam and Mahodand Lake
  • Dec to Feb snow and skiing at Malam Jabba
Plan this trip
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Naran and Kaghan

Saif ul Malook Lake sits in a bowl of peaks, and the Kunhar river runs beside the road. Lulusar Lake and Babusar Top add high-altitude views.

  • Jun to Sep green valley, lakes and Babusar Top
  • Oct to Nov autumn colour and quiet roads
  • Dec to Feb snow around Naran (check road status)

Azad Kashmir

Neelum Valley

Waterfalls, a blue-green river and wooden villages. Ride the cable car and walk up to Arang Kel, a village on a ridge above the valley.

  • May to Jun waterfalls at full flow
  • Jul to Sep green hills and riverside stays
  • Oct to Nov autumn colours
Plan this trip
Chitral

Kalash Valleys

Three green valleys, Bumburet, Rumbur and Birir, with wooden houses and the colourful dress of the Kalash people. Festivals fill them with music and dance.

  • Mid May Chilimjusht spring festival
  • Late Aug Uchau harvest festival
  • December Chaumos winter festival and snow
Plan this trip
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Shogran and Siri Paye

A jeep ride from Shogran leads up to Siri Paye, a high meadow with a small lake and a wide view of the peaks. Mist drifts through the pines each morning.

  • Jun to Sep green meadows, mild weather
  • October autumn and cool mornings
  • Dec to Feb snow in Shogran (check road status)

Gilgit-Baltistan

Passu and Attabad Lake

Attabad’s turquoise water sits between bare mountains, and the Passu Cones rise like sharp spires above the Hussaini suspension bridge.

  • Apr to May blossom and mild weather
  • Jun to Sep boating and glacier walks
  • October golden poplar and apricot trees
